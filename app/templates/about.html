About

This is an operational briefing on US AI regulation that may affect voice/conversation AI operators, customer-service bots, and phone AI — including disclosure, TCPA synthetic-voice, ADMT, and related themes. It is not legal advice. Status labels and summaries are curated for product and compliance awareness; always confirm against primary sources before decisions.

Data is refreshed monthly (about the 1st). Each jurisdiction page also links the specific statutes, bills, and agency pages behind that row. Federal coverage appears as its own card; states and DC are on the map and list.

Where the information comes from

Everything below is free and public. This tracker does not depend on paid legal databases, MultiState, Westlaw, or a licensed scraper — just primary government and legislature sites, plus NCSL’s public AI legislation database as a cross-check.

Federal

  • FCC Declaratory Ruling FCC 24-17 Confirms AI-generated voices count as artificial/prerecorded under the TCPA.
  • 47 U.S.C. § 227 (TCPA) The statute behind consent and robocall rules for phone AI.
  • 15 U.S.C. § 45 (FTC Act § 5) Unfair or deceptive acts — including misleading people about bots or AI capabilities.
  • FTC chatbot inquiry Agency attention on AI chatbots that act like companions or people.
  • Federal Register · EO 14365 Federal AI policy / preemption narrative — does not erase state duties on its own.

Priority states & local

  • California LegInfo Bot Act, AB 1609 status, and related bill text.
  • CPPA · CCPA / ADMT updates Automated decision-making technology rules under California privacy law.
  • CA Civil Rights Council · FEHA ADS Employment automated-decision rules for California workers.
  • Colorado SB 26-189 ADMT duties for consequential decisions, including residential lease/purchase.
  • Connecticut PA 26-15 (PDF) Online Safety / AI Responsibility waves (2026 and 2027 effective dates).
  • Texas HB 149 (TRAIGA) Statewide AI governance act and legislative history.
  • Utah SB 149 (AI Policy Act) Generative-AI disclosure when a consumer asks (and stronger duties in some contexts).
  • Maine 10 M.R.S. § 1500-DD Required disclosure of AI chatbots to Maine consumers.
  • Illinois AI Video Interview Act Notice and consent when AI is used on employment video interviews.
  • NYC DCWP · Local Law 144 Bias audits and candidate notice for automated employment decision tools in NYC.
